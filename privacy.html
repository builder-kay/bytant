Privacy Policy

Effective September 16, 2026. This policy explains how Bytant Innovative Group handles information shared through this website.

Information you choose to share

The website does not send project inquiry form data to a Bytant database. When you submit the form, your browser prepares a WhatsApp message containing the details you entered. You decide whether to send that message. You may also contact us directly by email.

How we use information

Information sent to us may be used to respond to inquiries, evaluate potential projects or partnerships, provide requested services, and maintain relevant business records.

Third-party services

The website links to services including WhatsApp, GitHub, LinkedIn, YouTube, and Bytant products. Those services process information under their own privacy policies.

Data protection

We aim to handle personal and business information responsibly and limit access to people who need it for the relevant conversation or work. Please do not submit confidential credentials, passwords, financial records, or sensitive personal data through the inquiry form.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete personal information you have shared with us, subject to legitimate legal or business record requirements.
